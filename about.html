About Me

Welcome to Reel Talk! This site is my personal space for reviews, analysis, and commentary on movies, games, and comics. I care deeply about storytelling, whether it comes from a big studio release, a cult favorite, or something that makes you stop and think, "What did I just watch?" Reel Talk started as a fun creative outlet and slowly turned into something more intentional. Over time, it became a place to dig a little deeper into why stories work, why they don't, and why some stick with us long after the credits roll. I try to approach everything here with honesty, curiosity, and just enough humor to keep things grounded.

Here on Reel Talk, you'll find:

  • Movie reviews covering both classic and contemporary films
  • Game reviews with a focus on narrative, design, and overall experience
  • Comic reviews exploring different eras, styles, and creators
  • Commentary, featuring articles and editorials on film, pop culture, and storytelling

The Story So Far

What started as a small creative experiment has grown into a long-term passion project:

  • 2023: Began writing about films purely for fun and to organize my thoughts
  • 2024: Refined my voice and structure, expanding into games and comics
  • 2025: Built this website from scratch to give all my writing a permanent home
  • 2026: Continues to evolve as an ongoing, independent project

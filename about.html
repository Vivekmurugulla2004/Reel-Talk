About Reel Talk

Reel Talk is a place for honest opinions about movies, comics, games, and the stories behind them. Every review here comes from actually finishing the thing and having a real reaction to it - not a synopsis, not a diplomatic list of pros and cons, just an argument for why it works or why it doesn't.

Here on Reel Talk, you'll find:

  • Movie reviews covering both classics and new releases
  • Comic reviews spanning different eras, creators, and styles
  • Game reviews focused on narrative, design, and overall experience
  • Articles and rankings for the arguments too big to fit inside a single review
  • Collections that group everything by mood or theme instead of by date

The Rating

The rating is simple: stars out of five. That's the whole system. Everything else on a review page - a favorite scene, a standout performance, a piece of trivia - is there because it's worth knowing, not because you need it to understand the score.

Why Movies, Comics, and Games Share a Site

They're telling the same kinds of stories through different tools, and a good story is worth talking about no matter which format it showed up in. That's the only real editorial rule here - no comment section, no algorithm, just reviews written by someone who cares more about getting the argument right than being nice about it.
